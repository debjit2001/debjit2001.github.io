About Me

I am currently a MS Research Fellow at Indian Institute of Technology, Kharagpur. My research focuses on ubiquitous computing and human activity recognition, with a specific emphasis on WiFi sensing and LLM-based reasoning for anomaly detection in complex sensing environments.

Prior to joining IIT Kharagpur, I worked as a Software Engineer at Persistent Systems, where I developed features and services using Spring Boot for enterprise banking clients. I hold a B.Tech in Information Technology from Academy of Technology.
